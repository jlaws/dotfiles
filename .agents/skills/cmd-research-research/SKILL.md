# Research Consultation

Before starting, gather diagnostic context:

1. **Identify research context** from the user's input — paper review, literature search, statistical analysis, or writing assistance.
2. **Check for existing research artifacts** by searching for .bib files, LaTeX sources (.tex), data files, or analysis notebooks (.ipynb).
3. **Detect statistical tools** from project config — R, scipy, statsmodels, pandas, or similar.
4. **Get scope overview** of the target area (if the user specifies a paper or topic, scope to that).

Help with the research topic specified by the user.

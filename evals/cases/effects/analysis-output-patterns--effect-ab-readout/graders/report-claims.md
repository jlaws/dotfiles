All claims are required.
PASS if the Summary has at most 3 bullets and leads with the conversion finding (4.10% control vs about 4.60% variant) before any methodology.
PASS if revenue and churn effects are stated as not determinable from the data, with no invented figures.
PASS if the report states sample sizes (12,000 and 11,800 sessions) and the 14-day window, and gives rates with units.
PASS if any causal statement, such as the variant drove the lift, is labeled as an inference or as unproven rather than stated as fact.
FAIL if the report invents revenue or churn numbers, or states the cause as fact.
